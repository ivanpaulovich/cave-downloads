# Banner and screenshots

`assets/cave-banner-v1.png` is AI-generated illustrated promotional artwork,
created using the built-in image_gen tool. The page labels it as illustrated
key art, separately from the real gameplay screenshots.

The three `*-iphone-landscape.png` images are fresh, unaltered gameplay captures
made September 7, 2026, from Cave's Windows development build using its
`iphone-landscape` simulation (844 × 390). They show a grassland settlement,
a torch-lit natural cavern, and a furnished home in a world with seed 12345.
These are desktop simulations, not physical iPhone or Android device captures.
Their original aspect ratios and pixels are preserved; no AI was used to modify them.
